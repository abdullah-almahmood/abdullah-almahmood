### About

I study how people, machines and systems actually behave, and design the instruments that measure it. My work spans **digital healthcare and telemedicine**, **AI-powered motion detection**, **healthcare device design**, **environmental machine learning** and **generative AI**. I am an electrical and electronic engineer with six and a half years in applied engineering, and I am seeking a funded doctoral position from 2027.

### Doctoral research interests

| Theme | Question |
|---|---|
| Electric mobility | How electric vehicles change driving behaviour, adoption decisions and measured environmental outcomes |
| Digital health | Telemedicine adoption and trust; open-data early-warning systems for disease outbreaks |
| IoT devices | Connected healthcare devices that record behaviour and health signals as they happen |
| Trustworthy AI | Explainable machine learning and the social effects of generative AI |
